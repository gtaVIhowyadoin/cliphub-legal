# ClipHub Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

ClipHub is a personal, single-user tool run by **GTAVIHOWYADOIN** ("I") on my own computer. It
helps me make short clips from gameplay livestreams that creators have permitted me to use,
and post them to my own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not offered to the
public, and I am its only user.

## What ClipHub accesses
When I connect my own accounts, ClipHub receives, through each platform's official API:
- **TikTok:** my basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), permission to upload videos
  to my account, and a list of my public videos with their view/like/comment/share counts.
- **Instagram:** my professional account's basic profile, permission to publish media, and
  insights (views, reach, likes and similar) for my posts.
- **YouTube / Google:** permission to upload videos to my channel, read-only access to my
  channel's videos, and YouTube Analytics reports for my channel.
- **Twitch / YouTube public data:** public information about broadcasts by creators who gave
  me written permission (titles, dates, video files).

## How it is used
Only to upload clips I have approved to my own accounts and to show me how they perform. Data
is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.

## Where it is stored
Everything (access tokens, video files, statistics) is stored only on my own computer and is
reachable only from my own devices over a private network. Video statistics are refreshed
regularly. Cached platform data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days. Video files are
deleted automatically after they're no longer needed.

## Your choices and deletion
You can revoke ClipHub's access at any time:
- **Google:** https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
- **TikTok:** TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
- **Instagram:** Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites

Disconnecting an account in ClipHub deletes its stored tokens. To ask for any data about you
to be deleted (for example, if you are a creator whose broadcast was clipped), email
**gtaivhowyadoin@gmail.com**. I will respond within 7 days.

## Third-party terms
ClipHub uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of
Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy
(https://policies.google.com/privacy). Use of TikTok and Instagram is subject to their own
terms and privacy policies.

## Children
ClipHub is not directed at children and is not used by anyone under 18.
